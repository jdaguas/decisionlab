# DecisionLab V2 Plan

**Goal:** Make the app look as playful as it sounds, add accounts, and make the live demo smooth.

## 1. Design refresh
- **Colors:** aqua blue as the main color, with orange and green as accents.
- **Glass style:** frosted, see-through panels over a bright, colorful background. Use them
  for the header, the verdict, and pop-ups. Keep the work areas solid so text stays readable.
- Perhaps incorporated some animations/transitions
- Make fonts, colors, and spacing consistent across the app.
- Polish the key screens: landing page, workspace, the phone voting page, and the verdict.

## 2. Accounts (Clerk)
- Signing in is **optional**. You can still use the app as a guest, and people voting never
  need an account.
- Signed-in users get their dilemmas saved to the database, so they can open them on any
  device and keep control of their polls.
- When someone signs in, offer to move the dilemmas already saved in this browser to their account.

## 3. QR code
- A QR code for each poll, plus a big-screen live results view for the demo.
- See application as a group activity so be able to screenshare or cast

## 4. Testing
- Test V2 with more people (aim for 5+), including your brother for real.
- Security review, focused on the new parts: accounts, who can see whose dilemmas, and the
  QR/live results pages.
