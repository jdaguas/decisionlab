# Tester testimonials

Three people tried DecisionLab. Roy's and Mom's notes come from real test sessions. My
brother's is a **placeholder I made up**; swap in his real reaction once he's tried it.

---

## 1. Roy: which neighborhood to move to

**What he decided:** `Which neighborhood should I move to?`

**Options:** `Uptown`, `South Loop`, `Andersonville`. He attached a Zillow listing to each.

**Features used:** link options. He pasted Zillow URLs so friends could see the actual
apartments, not just neighborhood names.

> "I wanted to compare actual apartments, not just neighborhood names. Being able to paste
> the Zillow links right into the options meant everyone voting could click through and see
> what I was looking at."
> (Roy)

**What came of it:** link options were added because of Roy's request. He complimented the pros/cons feature and having multiple tools with multiple verdicts to feel like he's getting a variety of opinions.

---

## 2. Mom: peer review only

**What she did:** opened a peer review link on her phone and voted. She didn't use any of the
analysis tools.

**Features used:** Peer Review voting.

> "It was easy. I just tapped my choice and that was it. I wondered if I could see pictures of
> the options in the poll. And it would be nice if I could rank them instead of only picking
> one."
> (Mom)

**What came of it:**
- *Easy to vote:* this is the main result. Someone who isn't comfortable with technology
  finished peer review without help.
- *Seeing the options:* this already works. Options added as photos appear as large picture
  cards on the voting page. Her poll used text options, so she didn't see them.
- *Ranking:* She suggested the option of ranking options, but voting stays one pick per person.

---

## 3. My brother: 

**What he decided:** `Should I buy a Tesla?`

**Options:** `Buy the Tesla`, `Keep my current car`

**Context:** he's moving to the Bay Area, where he'll spend a lot of time in traffic, and there
are plenty of EV chargers around.

**Features used:** Decision Tree. Under the Tesla option he added
`Cost → Monthly payment`, `Cost → Insurance`, `Charging → Lots of chargers around the Bay`
(🤩 +2), `Commute → Hours in Bay Area traffic` (🙂 +1, since it's nicer to sit in traffic in
the Tesla), and a what-if branch, `Gas prices go up` (50%, 🤩 +2).

> "Charging turned out to be a non-issue because there are chargers everywhere in the Bay. Once
> I mapped out how much time I'll spend in traffic, the Tesla branch came out way ahead."
> (Brother)

**What came of it:** He never used a decision tree before, so that was a welcome and novel change to his decision making process.
